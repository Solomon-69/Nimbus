# Changelog

## 1.1.3 (3 Nov 2026)
- Night sky: one faded moon in the corner. No more stars, white dots or white blurs.

## 1.1.2 (3 Nov 2026)
- Fix: the Updates row in Settings no longer cuts its text off ("You have the newest v..."); the message now wraps to as many lines as it needs.

## 1.1.1 (3 Nov 2026)
- Places slides in from the left and Settings from the right, with a smoother glide, instead of both coming from the right.
- The top-left button is now a list icon, since it opens your list of places.
- The menu bar icon stays highlighted while the panel is open, like the other apps.

## 1.1.0 (3 Nov 2026)
- Nimbus is now called Weather. The app, its download files and its web page all use the new name; your places, settings and login item carry over, and older versions update to it automatically.
- New app icon: one soft cloud in front of the sun.

## 1.0.6 (3 Nov 2026)
- The panel now grows out of the menu bar icon (it grew from the left side before) and keeps the arrow tip pinned under the icon the whole time.
- No dark empty box while it opens or closes, and the weather cards are there from the first frame instead of fading in late.

## 1.0.5 (3 Nov 2026)
- The arrow now has the same shape as the other menu bar apps (system popover silhouette: wider, lower, straight sides, rounded tip).
- Opening no longer flashes a dark blank panel or an old picture when the Mac is busy: the panel fades in from fully transparent while it pops out of the arrow.

## 1.0.4 (3 Nov 2026)
- The panel's top now blends into the arrow with the sky gradient in light and dark, instead of a grey separation. It opens and closes with the same pop (grow from the arrow, small overshoot) as the system popover Ludus uses, and clicking the icon again closes it.

## 1.0.3 (3 Nov 2026)
- The panel is now a real system popover, so it opens and closes with the same animation as Ludus and the other menu bar apps.
- Clicking the menu bar icon again closes it.
- It always reopens on the main screen and shows the fresh screen, never a stale picture.

## 1.0.2 (3 Nov 2026)
- Fix: the panel could open blank for a moment; the screen is now always visible and just settles in.

## 1.0.1 (3 Nov 2026)
- The hourly forecast is now at the top, followed by rain, wind, UV and sunrise; the old top card is gone.
- Sunrise bar is a round pill with a sun marker.
- Saved places are removed by swiping the row to the left (no delete button).
- Settings can be driven from the keyboard: Up/Down to move, Left/Right or Space to change, Esc to go back.
- The menu bar icon no longer bounces.
- Settings has a Check for updates row.

## 1.0.0 (3 Nov 2026)
First public release. Mac only. Live Open-Meteo forecast, unlimited places, moving sky, rain alerts, frosted-glass panel.
