# Changelog

## 1.0.1 (3 Nov 2026)
- The hourly forecast is now at the top, followed by rain, wind, UV and sunrise; the old top card is gone.
- Sunrise bar is a round pill with a sun marker.
- Saved places are removed by swiping the row to the left (no delete button).
- Settings can be driven from the keyboard: Up/Down to move, Left/Right or Space to change, Esc to go back.
- The menu bar icon no longer bounces.
- Opening the panel fades the fresh screen in, so no stale picture shows.
- Settings has a Check for updates row.

## 1.0.0 (3 Nov 2026)
First public release. Mac only. Live Open-Meteo forecast, unlimited places, moving sky, rain alerts, frosted-glass panel.
