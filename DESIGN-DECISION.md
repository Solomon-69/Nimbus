# Layout pick (27 Oct 2026, council quick mode, 3 advisors)

Options: A classic stack, B detail cards, C tabs.
Votes: Red Team picked A, Executor picked C, First Principles picked B (hybrid).

Chosen: **B hybrid**. Hero with a quiet warm glow, a 2x2 grid of icon-tile cards (rain chance, wind, UV, sun times), an air quality row, 24 hour strip, 10 day range bars, all in one scroll with a fixed header and footer. Places and Settings open as slide-up sheets from the header buttons, so the main view stays uncluttered.

Dissent kept:
- Red Team: the glow looks busy at 380 pt and four tiles hold only four facts. So the glow stays very soft and air quality gets its own row.
- Executor: places and settings need their own screens. They get sheets, not extra rows in the scroll.
